WELCOME TO THE WORLD OF NEDMAN

A classic ASCII adventure originally built for TI-83+ graphing calculators brought to real hardware!

The original Nedman was built using TI-BASIC during math class when my homework was done during the lecture and I had nothing better to do with my time.

The games star Nedman, a Theta who wakes up one day with his world in shambles. The dreaded PI and his minions threaten Nedman's very existence, And now Nedman has to fight, not only for his life, but for what remains of his world! But in order to do so he must unlock the power to defeat his enemies!

And maybe make some friends along the way?

Discover Nedman's powers as he discovers new places, realms, and worlds!
